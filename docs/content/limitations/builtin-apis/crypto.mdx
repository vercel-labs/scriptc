- **Crypto:** Hashing, HMAC, PBKDF2, and HKDF support every digest `crypto.getHashes()` lists in Node 24, with OpenSSL's aliases and object identifiers.
  Hash and Hmac accept every Buffer encoding and any TypedArray or DataView, `createHash` and `hash.copy` take `outputLength`, and `crypto.hash` takes its options object; HMAC, PBKDF2, and HKDF reject the SHAKE and KMAC digests with Node's errors.
  Hash and Hmac values are also Transforms: they can be written, piped into, used as `pipeline()` stages, read, and listened to, and they report Node's errors after the stream flushes.
  `copy()` of a Hash whose stream flushed, and stream use of a value after its stream closed, throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Through `unknown` and `any` slots and untyped JavaScript, Hash and Hmac values keep their identity and support `update()`, `digest()`, and Hash's `copy()`, called or read as values; their stream and event members there throw a coded SC2020 error.
  `timingSafeEqual` compares any view or ArrayBuffer.
  Secret KeyObjects (`createSecretKey`, `generateKey` and `generateKeySync` for `hmac` and `aes`) support `type`, `symmetricKeySize`, `export()`, `equals()`, and `instanceof KeyObject`, and createHmac, hkdf, createCipheriv, and createDecipheriv accept them; `KeyObject.from()` and `toCryptoKey()` are not available.
  `randomFill`, `randomFillSync` (every view kind and ArrayBuffer), `randomInt`, `randomUUID`, and `randomUUIDv7` follow Node 24.22, including callback timing; a `randomInt` callback whose error parameter is typed `Error | null` receives `null` where Node passes `undefined`.
  Scrypt supports its default parameters, numeric options, aliases, and memory limit.
  Both KDFs accept strings, Buffers, numeric typed arrays, DataViews, and fixed-length ArrayBuffers.
  `createCipheriv` and `createDecipheriv` support aes-128, aes-192, and aes-256 in CBC, CTR, GCM, CCM, ECB, CFB, CFB8, CFB1, and OFB modes and as key wrap (the wrap and wrap-pad ciphers and their -inv forms), des-ede3 in CBC, ECB, CFB, CFB8, CFB1, and OFB modes, des-ede in CBC, ECB, CFB, and OFB modes, chacha20, and chacha20-poly1305, on native targets, through `update()`, `final()`, `setAAD()`, `setAutoPadding()`, `getAuthTag()`, and `setAuthTag()`; other ciphers and wasm32-wasi builds are refused.
  `getCipherInfo` answers Node's info for every cipher OpenSSL names, including the ones without a native implementation.
  `getCipherInfo` options whose class defines `keyLength` or `ivLength` throw a coded SC2020 error.
  The result is Node's object; the SIV ciphers and the SM4 GCM, CCM, and XTS ciphers have no `nid`, and reading it where the declared `CipherInfo` type gives `number` throws a TypeError.
  Cipheriv and Decipheriv values are also Transforms: they can be written, piped into, used as `pipeline()` stages, read, iterated with `for await`, and listened to, and their stream properties read as in Node; written chunks go through `update()`, and the end pushes `final()` or reports its error through `'error'`.
  Through `unknown` and `any` slots and untyped JavaScript, in a program that uses streams, their stream properties and their `on`, `once`, `off`, `pipe`, `unpipe`, `read`, `pause`, `resume`, `setEncoding`, `write`, `end`, `destroy`, `cork`, and `uncork` members work; `write` and `end` take no encoding or callback there, and the other stream members throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Hash, Hmac, Cipheriv, and Decipheriv streams count a written string's bytes in `writableLength`, where Node counts the string's length.
  Decrypting chacha20-poly1305 without an authentication tag fails in `final()`; Node 24.22 returns unverified plaintext.
  After `update()` fails, for example past the AES-GCM limit of 2^36 - 32 bytes per message, every later call on that cipher throws; Node still lets `final()` run.
  An output encoding that is a function or an instance of a class defining `toString` throws a coded SC2020 error.
  `createECDH` and `ECDH.convertKey` support prime192v1, secp224r1, prime256v1, secp384r1, secp521r1, secp192k1, secp224k1, secp256k1, brainpoolP256r1, brainpoolP384r1, and brainpoolP512r1, on native targets, through `generateKeys()`, `computeSecret()`, `getPublicKey()`, `getPrivateKey()`, `setPrivateKey()`, and `setPublicKey()`, in compressed, uncompressed, and hybrid forms; the other curves `crypto.getCurves()` lists and wasm32-wasi builds are refused.
  A call on an ECDH value whose arguments no @types/node overload accepts (`getPublicKey("HEX")`) is refused at compile time, as is `computeSecret(view, encoding)`: Node reads that encoding as the input encoding and answers a Buffer where @types/node declares a string.
  ECDH property reads and writes throw a coded SC2020 error.
  `createDiffieHellman`, `getDiffieHellman`, and `createDiffieHellmanGroup` support the modp1, modp2, modp5, and modp14 through modp18 groups, explicit primes and generators, and generated safe primes, on native targets, through `generateKeys()`, `computeSecret()`, `getPrime()`, `getGenerator()`, `getPublicKey()`, `getPrivateKey()`, `setPublicKey()`, `setPrivateKey()`, and `verifyError`, with Node's private key lengths, DH_check results, and errors; wasm32-wasi builds are refused.
  Primes longer than 8192 bits throw a coded SC2020 error; Node accepts primes up to 10000 bits for key generation and secrets.
  `setPublicKey()` and `setPrivateKey()` keys longer than 80000 bytes, not counting leading zero bytes, throw a coded SC2020 error, as does a generator that long with a prime below 512 bits; Node accepts them.
  A call on a DiffieHellman value whose arguments no @types/node overload accepts, or whose output encoding is typed `any`, is refused at compile time, as are its `Object.prototype` methods; other property reads, and property writes, throw a coded SC2020 error.
  `Object.keys()` and `in` do not see a DiffieHellman's own `verifyError` property, and `JSON.stringify()` and `instanceof` are refused.
  After `createDiffieHellman(primeLength)` throws `Invalid DH parameters`, the next OpenSSL error Node throws can list the failed generation's errors in `opensslErrorStack`; errors in a compiled binary list only their own.
  `crypto.diffieHellman` computes ECDH and X25519 secrets from EC and X25519 keys, given as KeyObjects or in any form `createPrivateKey` and `createPublicKey` read, with Node's errors; DH keys have no KeyObjects here, and the `DiffieHellman` and `DiffieHellmanGroup` constructors are not available.
  `checkPrime`, `checkPrimeSync`, `generatePrime`, and `generatePrimeSync` support bigint, ArrayBuffer, typed-array, Buffer, and DataView values and the `checks`, `safe`, `add`, `rem`, and `bigint` options on native targets, with Node's errors; the callback forms compute before they return and call back in a later macrotask.
  Candidates and sizes longer than 262144 bits throw a coded SC2020 error, and wasm32-wasi builds are refused.
  Node calls their callbacks with `undefined` as the error, which a callback whose error parameter is typed `Error | null`, as @types/node declares it, receives as `null`.
  RSA, RSA-PSS, EC, Ed25519, and X25519 private and public KeyObjects come from `generateKeyPairSync`, `generateKeyPair`, `createPrivateKey`, and `createPublicKey`, on native targets.
  `generateKeyPairSync` and `generateKeyPair` check their arguments in Node's order, with Node's errors, including the OpenSSL errors for RSA modulus lengths below 512 or above 2147483647 bits and for public exponents of 0, 1, or an even number, which `generateKeyPair` passes to its callback.
  RSA keys generate with modulus lengths from 512 to 8192 bits, an odd length from 2048 bits yielding one bit less as in Node, and odd public exponents up to 4294967295; longer moduli that Node accepts, and `paramEncoding: 'explicit'`, throw a coded SC2020 error.
  The EC curves are prime256v1 (P-256), secp384r1 (P-384), secp521r1 (P-521), and secp256k1.
  Keys read and write PEM and DER as PKCS#1, SEC1, PKCS#8, and SubjectPublicKeyInfo, and JWK (Ed25519 and X25519 keys use PKCS#8, SubjectPublicKeyInfo, and JWK); `createPublicKey` also takes a private key or an X.509 certificate.
  EC, Ed25519, and X25519 keys also read and write the `raw-public` and `raw-private` formats, including compressed EC points.
  Raw X448, Ed448, and post-quantum keys, EC scalars of 0 or at least the curve order, and the EC point at infinity, which Node accepts, throw a coded SC2020 error.
  They support `type`, `asymmetricKeyType`, `asymmetricKeyDetails`, `export()`, `equals()`, and `instanceof KeyObject`.
  `crypto.sign`, `crypto.verify`, `createSign`, and `createVerify` support RSA PKCS#1 v1.5 and PSS padding and ECDSA with DER and IEEE P1363 encodings over SHA-1 and SHA-2 digests, and Ed25519 through `crypto.sign` and `crypto.verify` with a `null` or `undefined` algorithm.
  RSA-PSS keys follow their RSASSA-PSS-params as Node does: PSS padding only, the key's digest and MGF1 digest, and salts of at least the key's length, with OpenSSL's errors; their details name the parameters, they export as PKCS#8 and SubjectPublicKeyInfo only, and digests other than SHA-1 and SHA-2 in their options throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Ed25519 signing, verification, and private keys link when a string literal in the program names `ed25519` (in any case); without one they throw a coded SC2020 error, while Ed25519 public keys still import and export.
  X509Certificate supports `publicKey`, `verify()`, `checkIssued()`, `checkPrivateKey()`, `checkHost()`, `checkEmail()`, `checkIP()`, `toLegacyObject()`, `toString()`, and `toJSON()`, and `String()`, template literals, and `+` give its PEM text as `toString()` does.
  `publicKey`, `verify()`, and `checkPrivateKey()` are refused in wasm32-wasi builds, and an Ed25519 certificate's `verify()` needs the `ed25519` literal like the other Ed25519 operations.
  Own-key walks over a certificate (`Object.keys()`, `Object.entries()`, spread, `for...in`, and `hasOwnProperty`) are refused, since Node keeps its fields as prototype getters.
  `JSON.stringify()` and `console.log()` of a certificate, a certificate stored in an `unknown` or `any` slot, and its methods on a union-typed value are also refused.
  Sign and Verify values accumulate `update()` data and are not writable streams.
  Private keys export encrypted (`cipher`, `passphrase`) with aes-128, aes-192, and aes-256 in CBC and ECB modes and with des-ede3-cbc, PKCS#8 as PBES2 with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and PKCS#1 and SEC1 as traditional PEM, and the ciphers OpenSSL refuses for a form throw its errors; a cipher option can also be an OID; other ciphers Node encrypts with throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Encrypted private keys import with a `passphrase` from PBES2 with HMAC-SHA PRFs and the CBC and ECB ciphers, and from traditional PEM with the CBC ciphers, with Node's errors for a missing or wrong passphrase; other schemes and ciphers throw a coded SC2020 error once a passphrase is given.
  The other key types (`dsa`, `ed448`, `x448`, and the rest) and wasm32-wasi builds throw a coded SC2020 error or are refused.
  Comparing keys of different types with `equals()` answers false; Node also leaves an OpenSSL error queued, which a later key operation can report.
  `publicEncrypt`/`privateDecrypt` remain unsupported.
  The WebCrypto `crypto` global, which node:crypto also exports as `webcrypto`, supports `randomUUID()` and `getRandomValues()`, also read as values from untyped JavaScript.
  Its `subtle` object, also node:crypto's `subtle` export, supports `generateKey()` and `exportKey()` for HMAC and AES keys, `importKey()` for HMAC, AES, PBKDF2, and HKDF keys, `encrypt()` and `decrypt()` with AES-GCM, AES-CBC, and AES-CTR and `wrapKey()` and `unwrapKey()` of secret keys with AES-KW or those ciphers on native targets, `deriveBits()` and `deriveKey()` with PBKDF2 and HKDF, HMAC `sign()` and `verify()`, and SHA-1 and SHA-2 `digest()`.
  Secret keys import and export in the `raw`, `raw-secret`, and `jwk` formats.
  `generateKey()`, `importKey()`, `exportKey()`, `sign()`, and `verify()` also support ECDSA (P-256, P-384, and P-521), Ed25519, RSASSA-PKCS1-v1_5, and RSA-PSS, with the `spki`, `pkcs8`, and `jwk` formats and `raw` for ECDSA and Ed25519 public keys; ECDSA, Ed25519, and RSA are refused in wasm32-wasi builds.
  ECDSA, Ed25519, and RSA link when a string literal in the program names the algorithm; a name built only at run time throws a coded SC2020 error.
  `wrapKey()` and `unwrapKey()` of ECDSA, Ed25519, and RSA keys throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Other SubtleCrypto methods, algorithms, and key formats are refused or throw a coded SC2020 error.
  Native derivations finish before their deferred callbacks, so computation blocks the caller.
