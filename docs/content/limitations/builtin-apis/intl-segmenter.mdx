- **Intl.Segmenter:** Unicode grapheme segmentation, iteration, and `containing()` compile, with the default locale or one language tag of the form language[-script][-region] written as a string literal (grapheme clusters do not depend on the locale).
  In JavaScript dependencies, word and sentence segmenters construct, and their `segment()` throws an SC2020 error.
  Locale negotiation over several locales, word and sentence segmentation, `resolvedOptions()`, and detached methods are unsupported.
